# Ball controls

_Back to the [Home page](Home.md)_

One item in the top menu, "Ball controls"

| Choice | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | The stick is not reversed while Glover stands on the ball. This is the default |
| Normal | The stick is reversed while Glover stands on the ball, as in the original game |

The choice is saved with the other settings _(see [Settings](Settings.md))_
