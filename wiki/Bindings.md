# Bindings

_Back to the [Home page](Home.md)_

Bindings are the button combinations that start quick actions. The menu has one item for each of these actions: Reset level, Warp, Last checkpoint, Go to checkpoint, Frame advance, Frame step, Slow motion, Hide HUD, Save state, Load state, Record input and Play recording

The default bindings are on the [Controls](Controls.md) page

## Change a binding

1. Select the action and press A. The row shows `press`
2. Let go of every button. The change waits until you do
3. Press the buttons you want, then let go of them all

Combinations made on controller 1 include the stick direction that you hold, if any. If you don't press anything for a few seconds, the change is cancelled

To cancel at once, press B alone. The menu's own four C buttons can't be a binding either: they also cancel

A combination that another action has moves to the new action. The other action then has no binding

Press L on an action to clear its binding

Bindings trigger when their buttons are held and the last one has just been pressed. On controller 1, the bound buttons are hidden from the game while the binding triggers. A binding with the stick also hides the stick from the game until you let it go

The stick counts only when it points straight in one direction. A diagonal, like the corner notches of the stick, matches no binding

A plain game button (A, B, Z, R and so on) can be a binding, but the game then never sees that button

Frame step repeats while you hold its binding

Bindings are kept with Save settings _(see [Settings](Settings.md))_
