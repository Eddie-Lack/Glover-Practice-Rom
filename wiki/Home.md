# Glover Practice ROM

Practice the game or check out some cool stuff. It has savestates, collision viewer, level and checkpoint warps, timers, an input display, frame advance, some other things

Apply the patch to your own Glover (USA) ROM (see the [README](../README.md) for how)

## The Expansion Pak is required!!

## Pages

- [Controls](Controls.md): How to move around in the menu

| Page | Options |
