# Glover Practice ROM

Practice the game or check out some cool stuff. It has savestates, collision viewer, level and checkpoint warps, timers, an input display, frame advance, some other things

Apply the patch to your own Glover (USA) ROM (see the [README](../README.md) for how)

## The Expansion Pak is required!!

Without it the ROM shows "EXPANSION PAK REQUIRED" and stops. In an emulator, turn the Expansion Pak (8 MB of memory) on in its settings

## Quick start

1. Hold all four C buttons to open the practice menu. It works on the title screen too
2. Warp, Go: you are in Atlantis 1. Choose another level in the row above Go first
3. Stick held left + D-pad up saves a state. Stick held left + D-pad down loads it
4. Stick held up + D-pad up restarts the level
5. Settings, Save settings keeps your changes when the console is switched off

The other default quick actions are on the [Controls](Controls.md) page. What survives a power-off is on the [Settings](Settings.md#what-is-kept) page. Questions: see the [FAQ](FAQ.md)
