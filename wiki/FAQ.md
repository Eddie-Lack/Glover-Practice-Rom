# FAQ

_Back to the [Home page](Home.md)_

## My settings were gone after I switched the console off

Choose Settings, Save settings. Changes you don't save last until the console is switched off. The Status row says `saved` while the settings match the saved ones. _See [Settings](Settings.md#what-is-kept) for what is kept where_

## My savestates were gone after I switched the console off

States in the slots live in the Expansion Pak's memory and are lost at power-off. On a SummerCart64 you can keep them as files on its SD card _(see [Savestate](Savestate.md))_

## My time was "not kept: modified"

A tool that changes the game was used in that attempt, and the HUD shows `MODIFIED` while it counts. The list of tools is on the [Settings](Settings.md#practice-best-times) page. A saved "Controller 2: debug controls" counts too

## A savestate doesn't save or load

Savestates don't work on the title screens, and a save is refused while the screen fades for a warp, a reset or a level exit ("state not saved: game busy"). Save once the level runs

## Can I use the SD card of an EverDrive?

Not yet. The card files work only on a SummerCart64 for now. Everything else works on any flash cart that gives the ROM an Expansion Pak and an EEPROM

## The menu doesn't open

Hold all four C buttons on controller 1. Controller 2 can't open it

## How do I apply the patch without the web page?

The release has a BPS patch. Apply it with any BPS patcher, such as Floating IPS, to Glover (USA) in .z64 byte order
